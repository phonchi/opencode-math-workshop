  ┃   31 $$X_{\text{transformed}} = X_{\text{centered}} W_K^T = X_{\text{centered}} \cdot V[:K, :]^T$$                  
  ┃   32                                                                                                                
  ┃   33 ### 步驟五：解釋變異量 (Explained Variance)                                                                    
  ┃   34 每個主成分對應的變異量為：                                                                                     
  ┃   35 $$\sigma_i^2 = \frac{S_i^2}{N - 1}$$                                                                           
  ┃   36                                                                                                                
  ┃   37 累積解釋變異比例為：                                                                                           
  ┃   38 $$\text{Ratio}_k = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{k} S_i^2}{\sum_{j=1}^{D} S_j^2}$$                                         
  ┃   39
